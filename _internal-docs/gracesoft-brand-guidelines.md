## Dark Mode

Fonts stay the same in dark mode. The Purple and Black scales above are reused as-is — dark mode is a different assignment of roles to the same swatches, not a new palette. Shades below are chosen so every text/background pairing clears WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components); ratios are noted for reference.

### Surfaces

| Role | Shade | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Black 950 | #000000 |
| Background (softer alt) | Black 900 | #1a1a1a |
| Elevated / Card | Black 900 | #1a1a1a |
| Hover / Secondary surface | Black 800 | #333333 |
| Border / Divider | Black 700 | #4d4d4d |

Border / Divider is decorative only — it is not intended as a text-contrast pairing.

### Text

| Role | Shade | Hex | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Black 50 | #f5f5f5 | 11.6–19.3:1 |
| Secondary / Muted | Black 300 | #b3b3b3 | 6.0–10.0:1 |
| Disabled / Placeholder | Black 500 | #808080 | 3.2–5.3:1 (large text/UI only) |

Black 400 (#999999) — the shade that filled this role in the flat two-colour version of this palette — only reaches 4.4:1 on the Black 800 hover surface, just under the 4.5:1 body-text threshold. Black 300 is used instead so secondary text stays AA-safe on every surface above.

### Accent (Purple)

| Role | Shade | Hex | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text / Links / Icons | Purple 300 | #a79fe4 | 5.2–8.7:1 |
| Hover / Active text | Purple 200 | #c9c5ef | 7.6–12.7:1 |
| Primary button fill (+ Black 50 text) | Purple 600 | #372aac | 9.2:1 |
| Focus ring | Purple 300 | #a79fe4 | — |

Avoid Purple 400 and darker (500 / 600 / 700+) as text or icon colour directly on dark surfaces — contrast falls to roughly 1.3–4.3:1, below AA for body text.

## Logo and Wordmarks

### Company Logo and Wordmark

The company wordmark and icon are each supplied in three colourways: Brand Colours, Black, and White.

### Design Guidelines

#### Wordmark

Comprises of the company wordmark in full all caps using the Montserrat font in italics. "GRACE" uses 800, while "SOFT" uses 600 weights. On a new line, the full product name is spread out but does not overflow the width of the company logo.

Example: GraceSoft Sentinel.

#### Logo

The Logo and the website icon take the first 2 capital letters of the product name to form the logo. The colours follow the same pattern as the company logo.

Example: GraceSoft Sentinel—ST, in Brand Colours, Black, and White.
