# Sentinel Cook — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 September 2026
**Version:** 1.1.0

Sentinel Cook ("Cook", "we", "us") is an AI-powered chat assistant built by GraceSoft that identifies dishes from photos and generates recipes over WhatsApp and Telegram. This policy explains what we collect when you message Cook, why, and how to exercise your rights over that data.

## 1. What we collect

- **Your phone number or chat identifier** — whichever your messaging app (WhatsApp or Telegram) provides us as your sender ID, so we know who to reply to.
- **The content of your messages** — text you send us, including follow-up requests like "make it vegetarian."
- **Photos you send us** — the dish photo you upload, so Cook can identify the dish and generate a recipe.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect payment details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this chat.

## 2. Why we collect it

- To identify the dish in a photo you send.
- To generate a recipe (ingredients, method, substitutions, serving suggestions) and approximate nutritional information for that dish.
- To adjust a previously generated recipe when you ask for a dietary variation.

## 3. How your data is processed

- Your photo is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to identify the dish — we don't send your phone number along with it.
- The dish name is sent to the same AI provider to generate the recipe text.
- **Your photo itself is not stored** beyond what's needed to generate a reply — it's processed and discarded, not saved to a database. Our records log that a photo was received, not the photo's contents.
- Your conversation state (e.g. the most recent recipe, so a follow-up "make it vegetarian" works) is held in a short-lived session store and expires automatically after a limited period of inactivity.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **OpenAI** — processes your photo and text to identify dishes and generate recipes.
- **WhatsApp (Meta) / Telegram** — the messaging platform you're using delivers your messages to us and our replies to you, under its own privacy terms.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing purposes.

## 5. Retention

Photos are not retained after a reply is generated. Session state expires automatically after a short period of inactivity. Message logs (text only, not photo contents) are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for customer service and record-keeping, and are deleted on request (see below).

## 6. Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do any of these, contact:

**Email:** hello@gracesoft.dev
**Contact form:** https://gracesoft.dev/contact

We'll respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable time.

You can also delete your data yourself, straight from the chat: send **/deletemydata** and confirm when asked. That permanently deletes your conversation history and saved chat state with us. If anything can't be deleted automatically, you'll be told so and pointed to the contacts above.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Cook's features change. The version and effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version.
