# Sentinel Concierge — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 September 2026
**Version:** 1.1.0

Sentinel Concierge ("Concierge", "we", "us") is an AI-powered chat assistant built by GraceSoft that answers frequently asked questions and helps you book appointments over WhatsApp and Telegram. This policy explains what we collect when you message Concierge, why, and how to exercise your rights over that data.

## 1. You're talking to an AI

Concierge is an AI assistant, not a human team member. It tells you this at the start of every new conversation, and will confirm it directly any time you ask.

## 2. What we collect

- **Your phone number or chat identifier** — whichever your messaging app (WhatsApp or Telegram) provides us as your sender ID, so we know who to reply to.
- **The content of your messages** — the text you send us, so Concierge can answer your question or process your booking request.
- **Booking details** — the date, time, and duration of any appointment you request or confirm, which we write to the business's calendar.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect payment details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this chat.

## 3. Why we collect it

- To answer your questions, using the business's published FAQ knowledge base.
- To find and confirm available appointment slots on your behalf.
- To maintain a record of the conversation and any booking made, for customer service and operational purposes.

## 4. How your data is processed

- Message text is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a response. OpenAI processes this text under its own data-handling terms; we do not send them your phone number.
- Booking requests are sent to the business's Google Calendar to check availability and create appointments.
- Your conversation state (e.g. an in-progress booking) is held in a short-lived session store and expires automatically after a limited period of inactivity.
- A record of message text, direction (inbound/outbound), and any booking created is kept for customer service and record-keeping. We do not store the raw technical payload your messaging app sends us — only the message content and booking details described above.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **OpenAI** — processes message text to generate responses.
- **Google Calendar** — stores appointment bookings made through Concierge.
- **WhatsApp (Meta) / Telegram** — the messaging platform you're using delivers your messages to us and our replies to you, under its own privacy terms.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing purposes.

## 6. Retention

Session/conversation state used to track an in-progress booking expires automatically after a short period of inactivity. Message and booking records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, dispute resolution, and business record-keeping, and are deleted on request (see below).

## 7. Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do any of these, contact:

**Email:** hello@gracesoft.dev
**Contact form:** https://gracesoft.dev/contact

We'll respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable time.

You can also delete your data yourself, straight from the chat: send **/deletemydata** and confirm when asked. That permanently deletes your conversation history, your saved chat and booking-flow state, and our log of bookings you made in the chat. Appointments already on the business's calendar stay booked — ask Concierge to cancel them first if you want them gone. If anything can't be deleted automatically, you'll be told so and pointed to the contacts above.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Concierge's features change. The version and effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version.
